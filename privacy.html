2. What this policy covers

This policy covers:

  • this website, apexsoftcell.com;
  • our business contact with clients, prospective clients and visitors;
  • the development, testing and demonstration environments we operate for our own software, including our WhatsApp Business account.

What this policy does not cover

We are a software provider. When a broker or other client deploys our software, that client operates it under their own name, on their own infrastructure or hosting, with their own WhatsApp Business account and their own agreements with their customers.

In those deployments the client — not Apex Softcell — decides what personal data is collected and why, and the client's own privacy policy governs it. Where we process personal data on a client's behalf, we act only on that client's written instructions under our services agreement with them.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Through this website

  • Information you give us — your name, company, email address, telephone number and the content of your message when you contact us or request a demonstration.
  • Technical information — IP address, browser type, pages viewed and similar data recorded in ordinary server logs. We do not use Google Analytics or any comparable analytics service, and we do not use cookies to track you across websites.

3.2 Through our WhatsApp Business account

We operate a WhatsApp Business account to develop, test and demonstrate our account-opening software. If you message that number, we receive and process:

  • your WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name;
  • the content of the messages you send us, including any text, images or documents;
  • a record of what we sent you, and whether it was delivered.

This number is a testing and demonstration facility, not a live service. Do not send real identity documents, financial details or other sensitive personal information to it. If you are opening an account with a broker, use the channel that broker provides — not this one.

3.3 Software demonstrations and trials

Our software is designed to handle identity and financial information — including PAN, date of birth, bank account details, signatures and identity documents — on behalf of our clients. During development and testing we use synthetic or internal test data wherever possible. Where a client's real data is involved in support or implementation work, we handle it only under that client's instructions and our agreement with them.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who host our website, carry our email or otherwise support our systems. They act on our instructions and may use the information only to provide those services to us.
  • Meta Platforms — messages sent to or from our WhatsApp Business account pass through WhatsApp's infrastructure and are processed by Meta under its own terms.
  • Our clients, where the information relates to work we are performing for them.
  • Authorities, regulators or courts, where we are legally required to.

6. How long we keep it

We keep business enquiries and contact records for as long as we have, or reasonably expect to have, a business relationship with you, and afterwards only as long as needed to meet legal or contractual obligations.

Messages to our WhatsApp testing number are retained for 7 days and then deleted.

Data held on a client's behalf is retained according to that client's instructions and their own regulatory obligations.

7. How we protect it

  • Encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) for our website and our software's interfaces.
  • Access controls, so staff see only what their role requires.
  • Separation of development, testing and production environments.
  • Logging and monitoring of access to systems that hold personal data.

No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect information proportionately to its sensitivity.

8. Your rights

Subject to applicable law, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you may ask us to:

  • confirm what personal data we hold about you, and give you a copy;
  • correct data that is inaccurate, incomplete or out of date;
  • erase data we no longer have a lawful reason to keep;
  • withdraw consent, where our processing relies on consent;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you are unable to.

If the data was given to us by one of our clients, we will refer your request to that client, who is the right party to answer it, and we will tell you that we have done so.

8.1 Asking us to delete your data

To ask us to delete personal information we hold about you, email mangesh@apexsoftcell.com with the subject line “Data deletion request”, and tell us:

  • your name, and the email address or phone number you contacted us from;
  • which information you want deleted, if not all of it.

We will acknowledge within 5 working days and confirm once the deletion is done. If you messaged our WhatsApp testing number, those messages are deleted automatically after 7 days in any case.

If the information was given to us by one of our clients, we will pass your request to that client, who is the right party to act on it, and tell you that we have done so.
